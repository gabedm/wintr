GeoNames city suggestions
https://www.geonames.org/

Derived from the GeoNames cities15000, admin1CodesASCII and countryInfo
extracts downloaded on 31 October 2026. GeoNames contributors provide this
data under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Wintr retains public city IDs, names, ASCII names, country/admin1 codes and
population for local prefix matching and ranking. Names longer than 100
characters are omitted. The GB display label is shortened to UK. Coordinates,
alternate-name lists and other source columns are omitted.

Coverage is cities over 15,000 people or capitals. This is not an exhaustive
list of places, and listing a city does not imply concert availability.
You can still enter another city directly. No suggestion query goes to
GeoNames; the index is served with Wintr. The city/country you apply filters
the received event list on this device and sends no provider request.

GeoNames data is provided as is, without warranty of accuracy, timeliness
or completeness. Extract descriptions: https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/readme.txt
